The Architecture of Intent

Current discovery platforms prioritize ad-revenue over local impact. Magic City Connect solves this by utilizing a B-Corporation Framework that binds the platform's financial success to the city's 99 neighborhoods.

Strategic Implementation

The platform architecture focuses on three pillars: Authentic Discovery, Community Ownership, and Scalable Impact. Implementation follows a phased rollout starting with Birmingham's core tech ecosystem and expanding through localized neighborhood advocates.
